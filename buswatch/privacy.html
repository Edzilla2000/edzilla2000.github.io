Bus Watch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-08

Bus Watch is an independent Android app that displays school-bus positions

from the MyBusStop service operated by Southland Transportation.

What the app stores

  • Your MyBusStop email and password, saved in Android's encrypted secure storage on your device only, so the app can sign you in again. They are sent exclusively to https://www.mybusstop.ca over HTTPS. Signing out deletes them.
  • Preferences (selected route, your chosen stop, refresh interval, alert settings), stored locally on the device.

What the app does not do

  • Bus Watch operates no servers and has no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. Nothing about you or your child is collected by us.
  • Bus Watch does not access your device location.

Third parties

  • MyBusStop / Southland Transportation receives your login and route requests, exactly as their own website and app would. Their privacy policy governs that data.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers receive standard map-tile requests (your IP address and the map area you are viewing) to draw the map.
